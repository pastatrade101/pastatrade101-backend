================================================================================
PASTATRADE101 — WHATSAPP TEMPLATES FOR META SUBMISSION
================================================================================

Submit each template in WhatsApp Manager > Message templates > Create template.

BEFORE YOU START — the two things that decide whether these get approved:

1. CATEGORY = UTILITY (not Marketing) for all five.
   Utility templates deliver reliably, can be sent outside the 24-hour window,
   and do not get throttled. Marketing templates are rate-limited, need stricter
   opt-in, and drive blocks. That is why every CTA below is ONE soft line. If you
   add sales language, Meta will recategorise it as Marketing or reject it.

2. VARIABLES CANNOT CONTAIN A NEWLINE.
   Each {{n}} is a short phrase supplied by the backend. Do not restructure the
   templates so a variable spans lines — the send will fail at the API.

Template NAMES must match exactly, because you paste them into
Admin > Notifications against each rule. Lowercase, underscores only.

Submit the English template first. Then use "Add language" on the SAME template
to add Swahili — do NOT create a second template with a different name.

5. pastatrade_beating_btc        [Utility]   send BY HAND, not on a rule
================================================================================
Names the specific coins beating BTC.

Selecting it in Admin > WhatsApp FILLS THE BLANKS AUTOMATICALLY with the live
confirmed leaders and the current breadth. The coin picker underneath lets you
swap the selection before sending. (The name is matched loosely, so
pastatrade_alt_leaders or pastatrade_beating_btc both work.)

This one is deliberately MANUAL. The others fire on a state change the app
detects; which coins to name is a judgement call, and a rule that broadcast a
ticker list on a schedule would eventually name something that had already
rolled over.

--- BODY (English) ---
Pastatrade101 · Beating BTC

Confirmed stronger than BTC right now: {{1}}

Breadth: {{2}} of altcoins are outperforming.

See the full list and why: {{3}}

Past strength is not a promise. Not financial advice.

--- BODY (Swahili) ---
Pastatrade101 · Zinazoshinda BTC

Zilizothibitishwa kuwa na nguvu kuliko BTC sasa: {{1}}

Upana: {{2}} ya altcoins zinashinda.

Ona orodha kamili na sababu: {{3}}

Nguvu ya zamani si ahadi. Si ushauri wa kifedha.

--- VARIABLES ---
{{1}}  PUMP, ENA, SPX, ZEC, SOL
{{2}}  50%
{{3}}  https://pastatrade101.com/app/altcoin-btc-lab

--- SAMPLE FOR META ---
PUMP, ENA, SPX, ZEC, SOL | 50% | https://pastatrade101.com/app/altcoin-btc-lab

NOTES:
- Keep {{1}} to about 5 tickers. A long list reads as a shill, and WhatsApp
  truncates the preview.
- "Confirmed" is not decoration. The picker only marks a coin confirmed when the
  app's own checks agree: quality "Clean signal" AND "High confidence", meaning
  liquid, 180+ days of history, no abnormal spike, above the 200-day MA, and
  positive over both 30d and 90d. Untick "Confirmed only" and you can name a
  coin that is up 268% on three weeks of history — which is exactly the kind of
  message that costs trust when it reverses.
- The "Past strength is not a promise" line is doing real work here. This is the
  only template that names individual assets, so it is the one most likely to be
  read as a buy list.

================================================================================
OPTIONAL — the Premium line
================================================================================
If you want an upgrade nudge, add this as the LAST line of templates 1-3. One
line only. Do not add it to all four, and do not make it longer.

  English:  Premium members also get the AI's reading of what changed.
  Swahili:  Wanachama wa Premium hupata pia usomaji wa AI wa kilichobadilika.

WHY THIS WORKS AND STAYS UTILITY:
The alert delivers the FACT for free — genuinely useful on its own, so a free
member is not being teased with nothing. Premium delivers the MEANING. That is
already how your product is built (rule-based "Signal read" vs the AI
interpretation), so the line describes a real extra rather than a paywall on
something you just sent.

RECOMMENDATION: submit templates 1-5 WITHOUT the Premium line first and get them
approved. Add the line as a template edit afterwards. A rejected template is a
7-day wait; a rejected edit leaves the approved version live.


================================================================================
AFTER APPROVAL
================================================================================
1. Run database/notification-rules-seed.sql in Supabase.
2. Set CONNECT_API_KEY in the backend env. While it is blank the whole notifier
   is inert — rules are editable, nothing sends. That is the safe default.
3. In Admin > Notifications, paste each approved template name against its rule
   and switch the rule ON. Rules ship DISABLED with a null template on purpose:
   a rule with no template cannot send even if enabled.
4. Send yourself a manual announcement first. Confirm it arrives before enabling
   any rule that reaches the whole member list.

Per-member rate limits already seeded (the SQL sets these):
  risk.band_changed       72h between, max 1/day
  exit.threshold_crossed  168h between, max 1/day
  altcoin.signal          96h between, max 1/day
  report.published        24h between, max 1/day
  (pastatrade_alt_leaders has no rule — it is sent by hand from the composer.)
Plus WHATSAPP_MAX_PER_RUN (default 200) as a hard ceiling per tick that the
admin panel cannot raise.
